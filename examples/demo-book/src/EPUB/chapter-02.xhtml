2. The Tank

The upper tank was nearly full. Mara opened the outlet and water pushed cleanly into the first section of pipe.

09:05

Jo came up from the lower garden carrying an empty watering can. He cultivated the beds below the workshop and had been checking them since dawn. “Still dry,” he said. “Not even a trickle at the bottom.”

The three of them divided the line into sections. Ari would walk downhill and listen at each joint while Mara worked from the valve above. Jo would watch the outlet at the garden.

Somewhere between the workshop and the lower beds, the water was being stopped.
