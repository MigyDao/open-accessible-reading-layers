3. The Break

10:20

Ari brushed away a mat of dry leaves and exposed a coupling with a hairline split along one side. The soil beneath it was damp. The problem was no longer an unknown blockage; the line had a mechanical failure.

Mara checked the size, then headed back toward the workshop for a replacement. Ari and Jo cleared the shallow channel around the pipe so the damaged section could be reached without bending the line.

11:00

Mara returned with a new coupling, two clamps, and a short length of spare pipe. They cut away the cracked section and fitted the replacement.
