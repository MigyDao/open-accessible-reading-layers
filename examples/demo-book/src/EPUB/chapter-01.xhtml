1. Morning

08:10

Ari reached the hillside workshop with a notebook in one hand and a coil of spare tubing over one shoulder. The lower garden had stopped receiving water before sunrise, and the day was already warming.

The storage tank sat higher up the slope. From there, gravity should have carried water through a narrow line past the workshop and down to the beds. Ari opened the nearest valve. Nothing came through.

A metal tool clicked against the bench behind him. Ari turned toward the sound.

Mara, the mechanic who kept the workshop running, set down a spanner and looked toward the pipe. “Start at the tank,” she said. “If the tank is full, we follow the line downhill.”

Ari wrote the first note of the morning: Find where the flow stops. Then he followed Mara up the path.
